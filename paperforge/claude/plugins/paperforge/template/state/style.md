## Voice and register
- Person: first-person plural ("we").
- English variant: American.
- Tense: present for method and claims ("we propose", "the policy outputs");
  past for actions performed ("we trained for 5k iterations").
- Register: precise and direct; no marketing language.

## Terminology (canonical spellings)
| Use | Not |
|---|---|
| sim-to-real | sim to real, Sim2Real |
| state-of-the-art (adj.) / state of the art (noun) | SOTA in prose |
| reinforcement learning (RL after first use) | R.L. |

## Notation policy
- `manuscript/macros.tex` is the single source of truth. Define once, use the macro everywhere.
- Vectors bold lowercase, matrices bold uppercase, sets calligraphic.

## Banned or restricted phrases
- "novel" (max once, in the contributions list, if at all)
- "very", "utilize", "in order to", "it is worth noting that"
- Superlatives without a citation or a number.

## Sentence-level preferences
- Average sentence length target: <= 25 words; vary rhythm.
- One idea per sentence; no triple-nested clauses.
- Numbers: digits for measurements ("6 seeds", "0.42 m/s"); never start a sentence with a digit.
